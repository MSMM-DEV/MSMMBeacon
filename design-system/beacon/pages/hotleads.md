# Hot Leads — relationship-first follow-up workspace

## Purpose and current friction

This reachable legacy page tracks warm conversations before a formal proposal: Engineering and AI opportunities, relationship context, a scheduled next meeting, expected value, participants and priority. Users need to identify the conversation, see what is next, update context and promote a qualified lead. The former type-first wide register buried identity and used tiny icon-only actions; its agenda clipped both lead and organization names. Notes lost content through character truncation.

## Priorities and organization

1. Keep the upcoming AI/Engineering agenda, with full meeting and company names, visible date/time and a clear details affordance. Preserve its scheduled/future filters and five-item cap exactly.
2. Make the register identity-first, followed by next date and rating, organization/type, anticipated value and attendees. Keep optional notes available through existing Columns control, now rendered without character truncation.
3. Offer a labeled Details action for pointer, keyboard and touch. Keep promotion visible and distinctly labeled; preserve reminders, deletion and restoration, including all callbacks and gates.
4. Retain existing filters, sorting, selection, inline editing and exports. No new business states or invented financial metrics.

## Visual and interaction contract

Shared cool-white/graphite theme, cobalt interaction color, solid data rows and gently frosted agenda chrome. Readable 14px titles, 12px minimum metadata, wrapping long strings, visible focus rings, non-shifting hover feedback and reduced-motion support. On phones the register becomes labeled records while the same fields/edit controls/actions remain accessible. Parent integrates CSS and performs browser verification.

## Skill use

UI-UX-Pro-Max's verified Web Long Token Wrapping guidance informs shrinkable text containers and overflow-wrap rather than clipped prose. Existing Lucide icons and shared React/Radix controls are retained for consistency.
