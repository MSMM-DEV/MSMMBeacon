# Team Calendar

## Purpose and user tasks
Employees compare colleagues' Outlook schedules before arranging meetings. They choose the relevant people, navigate to a date, compare time commitments and open an event for its time, location, owner and attendee responses. Calendar authoring remains in Outlook.

## Information priority
1. Selected people and the visible date range establish the scope of every result.
2. Event name, owner and time establish the commitment. Color reinforces identity; names and initials remain present.
3. Availability patterns matter in People views; full event details and attendee responses belong in readable dialogs.
4. Freshness and read-only provenance remain visible without displacing scheduling controls.

## Actions and organization
People selection is the principal page control, with search, department and location filters together inside the picker. The selected roster wraps with complete names and retains its existing expansion/disclosure behavior. Date navigation stays adjacent to the prominent range; period and layout switches have visible labels. Refresh stays at the end of the toolbar. Calendar content has its own bounded scroll area; the mobile agenda keeps complete event names and locations readable.

## Presentation
Use the master cool-white / graphite system. A translucent neutral toolbar sits above an opaque working calendar. Calendar dates, row headings and event dialogs receive a clearer type hierarchy. Person colors and the existing contrast-fitting computation are retained. Event and attendee text wraps in detail surfaces. Exact time-grid geometry and its density disclosures remain intact; compressed blocks open full detail.

## Interaction and accessibility
Retain all Radix focus management, Escape, selection persistence and existing date/view handlers. Use 44px mobile control targets, visible focus rings, and short opacity/transform transitions with reduced-motion opt-out. Never enlarge a fixed-time event beyond its real geometry; complete information is available through its existing detail dialog and agenda.

## Protected behavior
Fetching, privacy, permissions, sync, date arithmetic, density packing, attendee resolution, selections and event grouping are unchanged. No new writes, backend changes or data transformations.
